# Ownership Model Poster Series - Planning
## Overview
To kick off the semester, you and your team will be developing a series of infographic posters to communicate the different types of Ownership Models in business. Your goal as a team is to create a cohesive and united series of posters.


## Project Criteria
Your task in planning your posters is to:
* Assign each group member a type of business model to create an infographic for. Each member should create a poster for a different business model.
* Decide on colors, elements, and fonts that you will use to create posters that exemplify unity. 
* Research 3 examples of your type of business model and document them.

Your planning template will include:
* Group members names and business models
* Business Model Ownership Overview
    * Body text between 75 and 100 words
* How to Start
    * Body text between 50 and 75 words about how to set up that kind of business
* Pros and Cons
    * Body text between 50 and 75 words for pros
    * Body text between 50 and 75 words for cons
* 3 Examples of Your Business
    * Body text between 50 to 75 words for each example
    * 1 image for each example
* Colors (with hex codes)
    * Main Color (3-4 different variations)
    * Secondary Color (2-3 different variations)
    * Accent Color (1 variation)
* Fonts (include fontface, size, and weight!)
    * Heading font
    * Subheading font
    * Body text font
    * Caption font
    * Credits / Your name font
* Elements (often included in Canva)
    * Find 6 that would look good together

When it comes to poster creation, your poster will need to communicate:
* 3 examples of busineses of that type.
* At least 2 pros and 2 cons for that business model.
* An overview of what that ownership is.
* How to create a business that uses that type of ownership model.
